--- answer (scrolled) ---
If you are in danger right now, press SOS in DadaLink to alert your trusted contact, and call: Unified emergency number 911 · Police 999 · Ambulance (Saudi Red Crescent) 997 · Civil Defense (fire) 998.
I'm so sorry this is happening to you. It is not your fault.

If you are in danger right now
•  Press the SOS button, or call the police or emergency number shown in Help numbers. [2]

When you are safe
•  Keep evidence if you can. Take photos of injuries, and write down dates and what happened in your private log. [2]
•  Contact your embassy, a shelter or a support organisation from Help numbers. [2]
•  You can also complain to the labour ministry or the police. [2]
•  If you can, keep a clear photo of your passport, visa, residence card and contract in the private log. [1]

About your passport
•  Your passport is yours. A workers' rights guide says that in Saudi Arabia it is against the rules for an employer to keep it, except for a short time for official papers like your residence permit. [1]
•  Violence together with a kept passport can be warning signs of forced labour. If this is happening, you can tell your embassy or the police. [1][4]

You do not have to face this alone. This is general information, not legal advice. The embassy and the services in Help numbers can guide you on your case.
