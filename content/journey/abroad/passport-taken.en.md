Your passport is yours. It proves who you are. You need it to travel home, to get help and to change jobs. Many employers keep their workers' passports. This is not your fault, and you have the right to ask for it.

## What the law says

The rules are different in each country.

- **UAE:** The law says domestic workers have the right to keep their own passport and ID papers. Employers must respect this. [1]
- **Kuwait:** The domestic worker law of 2015 says employers must not take a worker's passport. [2]
- **Saudi Arabia:** A workers' rights guide says it is against the rules for an employer to take your passport, except for a short time for official papers like your residence permit. [3]
- **Lebanon:** Amnesty International found that the law does not clearly ban employers from keeping passports, and most workers it met did not have theirs. [4]

For the details in your country, see your country's rights page in DadaLink.

When an employer keeps your passport and you cannot get it back when you ask, this can be a sign of forced labour. The International Labour Organization (ILO) says this, because without papers a worker may feel she cannot leave or ask for help. [5]

## What you can do

1. **Keep copies.** If you can, take a clear photo of your passport, visa, residence card and contract. Save them in the private log in DadaLink. Send a copy to a family member you trust at home.
2. **Ask calmly.** You can say: "I would like to keep my passport with me, please. The law says it belongs to me." If you feel unsafe, do not argue. Your safety comes first.
3. **Write it down.** In the private log, write the date your passport was taken, who took it, and what they said. Add every time you asked for it back.
4. **Ask for help.** You can complain to the labour ministry in your country. You can also call your embassy. Find the numbers under Help in DadaLink.
5. **If your passport is lost or will not be returned,** your embassy may be able to give you an emergency travel document so you can go home. See "Contacting your embassy".

## Stay safe

Do not hand your passport or copies to people you do not know, even if they promise to help you. If someone keeps your passport and also locks you in, hurts you, or does not pay you, press **SOS** in DadaLink or call the emergency numbers in the Help directory.

## Where to get help

- Your embassy or consulate (numbers in Help in DadaLink)
- The labour ministry complaint line in your country (in Help)
- Your country's rights page in DadaLink
