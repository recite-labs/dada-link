When you are sick or hurt, it is hard to be far from home. You deserve care. Being sick is not your fault, and asking for a doctor is not asking for too much.

## In an emergency

Go to hospital or call for help **now** if you:

- Cannot breathe well
- Have very bad pain in your chest or belly
- Are bleeding a lot
- Fainted, or hit your head badly
- Have a high fever that does not go down
- Were hurt by someone

Press **SOS** in DadaLink, or call the ambulance number in the Help directory.

## What the law says

Rules are different in each country. Some examples:

- **Qatar:** Your employer must give you health care and medicines if you are sick or injured at work, without making you pay. Your employer must not make you work during your sick leave. [1]
- **UAE:** Your employer must give you medical care or health insurance, and pay compensation for work injuries. You can have up to 30 days of sick leave a year. [2]
- **Saudi Arabia:** News reports on the 2013 regulation say workers get up to 30 days of paid sick leave, based on medical reports. [3]
- **Kuwait:** The domestic worker law says the employer must provide medical treatment. [4]

For more, see your country's rights page in DadaLink.

## What you can do

1. **Tell your employer** you are sick or hurt, and ask to see a doctor. Say it clearly: "I need to see a doctor, please."
2. **Keep your papers.** Ask for a copy of any medical report, prescription or sick note. Take photos and save them in the private log in DadaLink.
3. **Write it down.** In the private log, write when you got sick or hurt, how it happened, and what your employer said.
4. **Rest when the doctor says.** Your health matters more than the housework.
5. **If your employer refuses care,** contact your embassy. Ethiopian embassies and consulates can help vulnerable workers get medical treatment, where they have the resources. [5]
6. **If you were hurt by someone,** you can also read "Abuse: what to do".

## Where to get help

- Ambulance and emergency numbers (in Help in DadaLink)
- Your embassy or consulate (in Help)
- Your country's rights page in DadaLink
