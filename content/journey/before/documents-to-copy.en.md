Papers can get lost, stolen or taken away. If you have copies, it is much easier to get help, prove who you are, and get new papers. Do this before you leave home.

## What to copy

Take a clear photo of each of these:

- your **passport** (the photo page and the visa page)
- your **visa** and any **work permit**
- your **contract**, every page [1]
- your Ethiopian **ID card** or kebele ID
- your **birth certificate** and any training certificates
- your **medical certificate**
- the **agency's licence**, name, address and phone number
- your **ticket**

Check that each photo is sharp and you can read every word.

## Write numbers on paper

Your phone can break, run out of battery, or be taken. Write these on a small piece of paper and keep it hidden in a safe place, like inside your clothes or bag:

- two or three family phone numbers
- your trusted person's number
- the Ethiopian embassy or consulate number in the country you are going to (find it in the **help directory** in this app)
- your agency's number
- your employer's name and address, when you know it

## Your papers belong to you

You have the right to keep your passport, residence permit and work permit in a safe place of your choice. [1] Fair recruitment rules say workers' identity documents and contracts should not be taken, destroyed or kept by others. [2]

Do not give your **original** passport or ID to a broker or to anyone you do not trust. If someone needs to see it, show it, or give a copy.

## What you can do

1. Take photos of all your papers. Do it today, not at the airport.
2. Print one set of copies. Give it to your trusted person at home.
3. Keep a second paper set in your bag, separate from the originals.
4. Save the photos in a safe online place with a strong password, such as your email. Then you can open them from any phone.
5. Write the important numbers on paper too.
6. If your passport is ever taken from you, read "My employer took my passport" in this app, and contact your embassy. Look in the **help directory** for the number.
