Your health is the most important thing you take with you. A little planning now can save you a lot of worry later.

## The medical check before you go

You must have a medical examination at a health centre chosen by the Ethiopian government, and get a medical certificate. [2] Under the law, the worker usually pays the medical exam fee and vaccination fee. [2]

If you are going to a Gulf country (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar), the check is part of the **Wafid** programme of the Gulf Health Council. Wafid checks that workers are fit to work and free from certain infectious and long-term illnesses. It uses clinical checks, lab tests and X-rays, and gives a health certificate with a barcode. [1]

Ask the clinic which tests they will do and what the results mean. You have the right to understand your own results.

## Existing illness and medicines

If you have an illness like asthma, diabetes, epilepsy, high blood pressure or HIV, or you take medicine every day, plan before you go.

- Ask your doctor for a **written prescription** in English.
- Ask your doctor whether your medicine is a **controlled medicine**.
- Some medicines that are legal in Ethiopia are **illegal** in some Gulf countries. For example, some medicines that are legal in other countries are illegal in Qatar. [4]
- In the UAE, you must carry a prescription for the medicine you bring. Controlled medicines need approval from the Ministry of Health **before** you travel. [3]

Never carry medicine for another person, even a small packet. Never carry anything if you do not know what it is.

## Health care abroad

Your employer should give you access to health care and medicine, and should buy life and disability insurance for you. [2] Ask your agency what health care and insurance your contract includes, and write it down.

## What you can do

1. Go to your medical check. Ask what each test is for.
2. Keep a photo of your medical certificate.
3. If you take medicine, get a prescription and ask if it is controlled.
4. Check the rules of your destination country before you pack any medicine. The embassy of that country can advise you. [3]
5. Ask your agency about health insurance and how to see a doctor abroad.
6. Think about contraception and sexual health before you go. Read "Contraception and sexual health before you go".
7. If you get sick abroad, read the "Sick or injured" article in this app and look in the **help directory**.
