Being far from home is easier when someone is watching out for you. A **trusted person** is someone at home you can always reach, who will act if you need help. A **safety plan** is what you agree together before you leave.

## Choose your trusted person

Pick someone who:

- you trust completely
- is easy to reach by phone
- will stay calm and act if you need help
- will keep your secrets

It can be a sister, mother, friend, or anyone you choose. You can choose two people.

## Agree your plan

Talk together before you leave. Agree on:

- **A code word.** A normal-sounding word that means "I am not safe, get help". For example, the name of a food or a relative. Only you two know it.
- **Check-in times.** For example, a short message every Friday. You have the right to contact your family regularly, at least once a week. [1]
- **What to do if you miss a check-in.** For example: wait one day, then call the agency, then contact the embassy.
- **Who to call.** Give them your agency's name and number, and the Ethiopian embassy's number in your country (find it in the **help directory**).

## Share where you are

When you arrive, send your trusted person:

- your employer's full name
- the address, area or a map location of the house
- a phone number for the house, if there is one

Give them copies of your passport, visa and contract too. Read "Copy your important documents".

## Use this app safely

- Add your trusted person in the app. The **SOS** button can alert them quickly.
- Use **disguise mode** to hide the app if someone checks your phone.
- Save important numbers on paper too, in case your phone is taken.
- Keep your phone charged. A small power bank can help.

## Know your rights

You have the right to contact your embassy or agency if you are mistreated or abused. [1] You have the right to keep your passport and papers in a safe place of your choice. [1] Nobody should take or keep your identity documents. [2]

## What you can do

1. Choose your trusted person this week.
2. Agree on a code word and check-in times.
3. Give them your agency's details and copies of your papers.
4. Send them your employer's address when you arrive.
5. Practise using **SOS** and disguise mode before you leave.
6. If you are ever in danger, use **SOS** or say your code word. Look in the **help directory** for emergency numbers.
