Going home is a big step. A little planning can protect your money and your rights. You do not have to do it alone.

## Collect what you are owed

- Ask for all your unpaid salary before you travel. Many workers go home without their wages. It is much harder to claim them once you are home [3][4].
- Ask about your end-of-service money. In Saudi Arabia, the 2013 domestic worker rules say that a worker who works four years in a row for the same employer gets one month's salary as an end-of-service benefit [1].
- Rules are different in each country. Read the rights page for your country in DadaLink.
- If your employer refuses to pay, try to complain before you leave, if it is safe. See "Unpaid wages".

## Who pays for the ticket?

In Saudi Arabia, the 2013 rules say the employer must pay for your ticket home when the contract ends. The employer must also pay if they end the contract without a legal reason, or if you leave for a legitimate reason [1].

Other countries have different rules. Check the rights page for the country where you work.

Do not sign any paper that says you were paid if you were not paid.

## Keep copies of your papers

Workers' groups say employers should let workers take copies of their work records, like pay records, when they leave [3]. Before you go, try to keep:

- your passport, visa and residence card (photos are fine)
- your contract
- pay slips and money transfer receipts
- screenshots of messages about your pay
- your employer's full name, phone number and address
- any complaint papers or reference numbers

Keep the photos on your phone and in an email account. Send copies to someone you trust.

## If you have no passport

Your embassy or consulate can give you a passport or a travel document. Helping citizens with passports and travel documents is one of the jobs of a consulate under international law [2]. Contact the Ethiopian embassy or consulate. You can find them in the Help numbers in DadaLink.

## What you can do

1. Write down how many months you were not paid, and how much.
2. Ask your employer for your final pay and any end-of-service money.
3. If they refuse, complain before you travel if it is safe. Ask the embassy for help.
4. Take photos of all your papers and keep them safe.
5. Tell a trusted person your flight date and time.
6. If you are in danger, use SOS in DadaLink.

Not being paid is not your fault. You did the work, and the money is yours.
